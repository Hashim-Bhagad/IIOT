# IIoT Study Studio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, explicitly requested by the user. TypeScript and local browser storage are implementation choices.

## Users

An NPTEL Industry 4.0 and IIoT learner starting from scratch, preparing within approximately 1.5 days. Assignment quizzes are the learner's main exam-preparation priority.

## Product Purpose

Teach the supplied course material with detailed visual weekly notes, then let the learner answer exercises and understand mistakes. Success means every supplied week and all 180 supplied assignment questions are covered, with explanations linking practice back to teaching.

## Operating Context

Twelve local Word documents cover Weeks 1–12. The user previously requested a short visual guide and now explicitly requests a detailed Next.js website. The short guide is content evidence, not a binding visual identity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Study material, definitions, comparisons, examples and diagrams for each supplied week.
- Exercise practice, feedback and revision using all 180 assignment prompts and answer keys.
- A separate PYQ practice area retains 279 additional questions from 360 source occurrences across 2023/2024, excluding weekly matches and combining repeated tested distinctions.
- Preserve original PYQ choices/keys and provenance; flag source conflicts and make ambiguous keys review-only.
- Most original answer-option lists are absent; generated distractors must be labelled as practice alternatives.
- Preserve course-key-specific facts as such and flag inconsistent or questionable statements.
- No official exam blueprint; do not invent course coverage or score guarantees.
- Local progress without an account is an implementation assumption. Hosting remains undecided; deliver a runnable local application.

## Evidence on Hand

Industry 4.0 and IIoT – Week 1–12 Notes.docx in the sibling `../course-docx/` folder, with 15 explained assignment questions per week. Week 12 includes a flagged UAV-generation key/slide discrepancy and two conveyor-case questions outside its transcript.

## Product Principles

1. Explain a concept before testing it.
2. Tie each exercise to a relevant lesson.
3. Preserve the difference between a general concept and a course answer key.
4. Use diagrams for relationships and tables for comparisons.
5. Keep progress persistent and feedback useful.
